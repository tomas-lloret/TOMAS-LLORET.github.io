I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Alicante with a background in Physics, Optics, and Photonics. My research focuses on **holography, holographic optical elements, photopolymer materials, and optical modelling**.

My work combines experimental and computational approaches to the design and characterization of optical systems, with particular interests in **holographic lenses, wavefront analysis, optical aberrations, and imaging applications**.

Alongside my research in optics and photonics, I teach in the area of **Applied Mathematics** and have an interest in mathematical modelling and systems theory.
