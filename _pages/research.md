My research focuses on **optics and photonics**, with particular emphasis on holography, holographic optical elements, photopolymer materials, and optical modelling.

## Research interests

### Holography & Holographic Optical Elements

Design, recording, characterization, and modelling of holographic optical elements, with particular interest in holographic lenses and their applications in imaging and light concentration.

### Photopolymers & Holographic Materials

Characterization of photopolymer materials for holographic recording, including their optical response, dimensional changes, stability, and influence on holographic performance.

### Optical Modelling & Wavefront Analysis

Modelling and characterization of optical systems using wavefront-based methods, with interests in optical aberrations, image quality, and computational approaches to optical design.

### Imaging & Visual Optics

Application of optical modelling and image-quality metrics to imaging systems and visual optics.

## Emerging research interests

### Mathematical Modelling & Systems Theory

Emerging research interests include mathematical approaches to complex systems, with particular emphasis on causality, nonlinear interactions, and whole–part relationships.
